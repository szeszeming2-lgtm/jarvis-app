Your personal AI assistant. It talks, remembers, and asks before it acts.

Common questions

  • Where do I get an API key? At console.anthropic.com → API Keys. Paste it in Jarvis → Settings → Anthropic. It's stored in your device's Keychain.
  • What does it cost? Jarvis uses your own Anthropic key, billed by Anthropic pay-as-you-go. A normal chat costs a fraction of a cent. You can set a monthly limit in the Anthropic console.
  • Reminders don't appear. Allow notifications: Settings → Notifications → Jarvis.
  • The microphone doesn't work. Allow it: Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and Speech Recognition → Jarvis.
  • Why do I have to tap Send or Call? Apple doesn't let apps send messages or place calls without your tap. Jarvis prepares them for you.
  • How do I stop Jarvis? Say or type “stop” or “Jarvis stop”, or tap Stop.
  • How do I remove an “Always allow” rule? Settings → Allowed without asking → Remove.
  • How do I delete my data? Settings → Erase everything. Deleting the app also deletes its data.
