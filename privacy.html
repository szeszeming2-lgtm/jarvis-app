Jarvis privacy policy

Jarvis is a personal assistant app. The developer of Jarvis does not run any servers and does not collect, see, sell, or share your data.

What leaves your device

  • What you type or say to Jarvis, plus the conversation so far and what you've asked Jarvis to remember, is sent to Anthropic (the company that makes Claude) to be answered. This uses your own Anthropic API key and is covered by Anthropic's terms and privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy).
  • If you ask about the weather, the city name is sent to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to get the forecast.
  • If web search is on, Claude may search the web for you through Anthropic.
  • Speech: when your device supports it, speech is turned into text on the device. Otherwise Apple's speech service is used, under Apple's privacy policy.

What stays on your device

Your memory, notes, to-dos, contacts saved in Jarvis, game scores, permission rules, settings, and the activity log are stored only on this device. Your API key is stored in the device's Keychain.

Permissions Jarvis may ask for

Microphone and speech recognition (to talk to Jarvis), notifications (reminders and timers), calendar (to read and add events when you ask), and folders you choose (for file abilities). Each is optional.

Your control

You can see everything Jarvis did in Settings › Activity log, and delete all Jarvis data in Settings › Erase everything. Deleting the app also deletes its data.

Children

Jarvis isn't directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open a question on GitHub, or see the support page.
